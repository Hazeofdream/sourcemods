
Hitting a Tank rock with a melee weapon reflects the rock back, accounts for proper ownership & angles
velocity.
 
Requires: left4dhooks

ConVars:
- parrythis_enable - 1/0 enable the plugin
- parrythis_damage - damage a reflected rock deals to what it hits
- parrythis_hitbox - radius (units) around a reflected rock that counts as hitting an infected
- parrythis_noshake - 1 blocks screen shake on the player who reflects a rock
- parrythis_debug- - 1 prints debug info to the swinging player's chat
